# Late redaction outage

## Setup

A fake model returns a synthetic email address, then the mandatory PII detector throws.

## Exercise

Capture the user-visible sink; inject the exception after generation and before output inspection completes.

## Expected evidence

No generated bytes are released; a fixed refusal and content-free DETECTOR_ERROR event appear. Recovery requires a healthy detector.

## Interview discussion

Why not fail open for availability? It breaks the declared data-boundary contract precisely when protection is unavailable.
