# False-positive review

## Setup

An English tutorial quotes an instruction-override phrase as educational material.

## Exercise

Record the rule and policy version, reproduce with a synthetic case, assess utility impact, change only development rules, then freeze and rerun held-out cases.

## Expected evidence

A visible false-positive record and an honest new confusion matrix; no suppressed failure or real payload logging.

## Interview discussion

Why not allow all quoted text? Attackers can quote malicious instructions too; quoting is context evidence, not blanket trust.
